# Privacy Policy

*Airplane Games, for iPhone and iPad. Effective November 22, 2026.*

**Airplane Games collects no data.** It has no network access, no accounts, no ads, no
analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that does.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your games in progress, your statistics (scores, times and streaks) and your
settings (sound, haptics and theme) on your device, so you can pick up where you left off.
This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it, and
**Settings → Reset All Statistics** in the app clears your statistics at any time.

## Links

The app's About screen has links to this project on GitHub. Tapping one opens your web
browser; GitHub's own privacy policy then applies to that website.

## Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new
effective date. The app is open source, so anyone can check what it does:
[github.com/guilhermechapiewski/airplane-games-app](https://github.com/guilhermechapiewski/airplane-games-app).
